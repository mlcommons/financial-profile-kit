## Precedence

1. The preprint *Agent Reliability Profiles in Financial Services* is the conceptual source.
2. `spec/SPEC.md` is normative for files; the schemas and conformance vectors implement it (`spec/SPEC.md` §1).
3. Reference tools carry no authority of their own.
4. The shared vocabulary is owned by the taxonomy repository; this Kit pins a release of it ([COMPATIBILITY.md](../COMPATIBILITY.md)).

## Regulatory observers

Supervisory authorities may take part as observers. Their participation does not constitute endorsement of the Kit or of any Profile produced with it.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers disclose and recuse where appropriate, consistent with MLCommons practice. A reviewer affiliated with a vendor, institution or assessor recuses from decisions that would specifically advantage their organisation's product or deployment.

## Code of conduct

All contributors and reviewers follow the [MLCommons Code of Conduct](https://mlcommons.org/policies/).
