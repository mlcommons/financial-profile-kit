Agent Reliability Profile Viewer

Spec arp/1.0-rc2 · Runs entirely in your browser, with no network requests: nothing you open here is uploaded.

Status: release candidate 2 of arp/1.0, prepared by the MLCommons Financial Services Working Group for pilot use and comment. Not a final MLCommons standard, certification scheme or endorsement. Field names and rules may change before 1.0. The public pilot is Level 1 (Asserted) only; Level 2 and 3 Profiles, including the Level 2 example, are for Working Group pilots with assessment support. The examples are fictional and signed with published DEMO-ONLY keys.

Paste or edit the Profile's JSON, then apply it. Everything the form does not cover (provenance, evidence, results, conflicts) is edited here.

Checker messages

"Well-formed" means the Profile follows arp/1.0-rc2: consistent, honestly labelled and, once issued, signed. It is not a judgement of the agent: it does not test whether the claims are true, whether the tests were adequate, or whether the agent is safe. A valid signature shows the signer held the key, not who the signer is. Workbooks and this form produce drafts: an owner issues a Profile by signing it with arp_conform.py sign. If this Viewer and the reference checker ever disagree, the reference checker governs.
